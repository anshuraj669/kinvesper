Kinvesper Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

Kinvesper is a voice companion for an older adult who lives alone, and for the family member who looks after them. It works through Alexa+ and a web dashboard for the caregiver. This policy explains what Kinvesper keeps, why, and who sees it.

What Kinvesper stores

  • Profile: the person's name and preferred name, the caregiver's name, relationship and phone number, the pharmacy name, and when the person last spoke to Kinvesper.
  • Medications: names, doses, daily times, instructions and pill counts.
  • Doses: which scheduled doses were taken, and when.
  • Check-ins: how the person said they feel, their words, and any symptoms mentioned.
  • Alerts and messages: missed doses, possible scams, symptoms, inactivity, and messages the person asked Kinvesper to pass to their caregiver.
  • Refill requests: which medication, pickup or delivery, and the request's status.

What Kinvesper does not receive

When you use Kinvesper through Alexa, Amazon handles your voice. Kinvesper receives only the request Alexa sends to it, such as "log my 8 AM Lisinopril", never audio recordings. Alexa's own handling of your voice is covered by Amazon's privacy notice.

How it is used

  • To answer the person's questions, such as whether a dose was taken.
  • To tell the caregiver, by text message, about missed doses, possible scams, worrying symptoms, long silences, and messages the person asked to send. Kinvesper asks the person to confirm before sending a message on their behalf.
  • To show the caregiver a dashboard and a weekly summary.

Kinvesper does not sell data, show advertising, or share data with anyone other than the caregiver listed in the profile and the service providers below.

Where it is kept

Data is stored in Amazon Web Services in the United States (Amazon DynamoDB), encrypted at rest. Text messages are sent with Amazon SNS. Sign-in for account linking uses Amazon Cognito. Access to the caregiver dashboard requires a private key.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Data is kept while the account is in use. To have it deleted, unlink Kinvesper in the Alexa app and ask us at github.com/anshuraj669/kinvesper/issues. We delete the account's data within 30 days.

Not for emergencies

Kinvesper is not a medical device and does not replace a doctor, pharmacist or emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
