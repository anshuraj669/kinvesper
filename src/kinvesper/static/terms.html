What Kinvesper is

Kinvesper helps an older adult keep track of medications, check in on how they feel, spot likely phone and email scams, and stay in touch with a family caregiver. It is offered free of charge, as is, as a project built for the Amazon Developer Hackathon 2026.

Not medical advice

Kinvesper is not a medical device. Reminders, dose records and scam checks can be wrong or late. Always follow the instructions of your doctor and pharmacist, and check your medication packaging. In an emergency, call 911. Kinvesper does not contact emergency services.

Your responsibilities

  • Enter medications and the caregiver's phone number accurately, and keep them up to date.
  • Only set up Kinvesper for yourself, or for someone who has agreed to it.
  • Keep the dashboard key private.

Text messages

The caregiver receives alerts by text message at the number in the profile. Standard message rates may apply. To stop them, ask us to remove the number.

Availability and liability

Kinvesper may change, be interrupted or stop at any time. To the extent the law allows, it is provided without warranties, and its makers are not liable for missed reminders, alerts that were not delivered, or decisions made using it.
